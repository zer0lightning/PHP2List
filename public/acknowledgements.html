Acknowledgements

Credit to the researchers who found PHP2Shell, and to NCSC-NL and the contributors who maintain the software list.

PHP2List would not exist without the people and organisations below. Thanking them does not mean they are involved with, or endorse, this unofficial site. All credit for the research and the software list belongs to them. The names here are as credited on php2shell.com and in the NCSC-NL/php2shell repository.

The software list

The inventory of potentially affected software is maintained by NCSC-NL, the National Cyber Security Centre of the Netherlands, together with everyone who has contributed entries, corrections and vendor information to the NCSC-NL/php2shell repository. See the full list of contributors on GitHub.

The list is published under the European Union Public Licence v1.2 (EUPL-1.2) and marked TLP:CLEAR. PHP2List displays it unmodified. Corrections should go to the source: see how to contribute.

As credited on php2shell.com, the researchers worked with the PHP security team and the Gemini team at Google on discovery, coordination and patch verification.
