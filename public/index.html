Watchlist

Add suppliers, specific products or keywords to narrow the list to what you use, or select ☆ Watch on any row to add that exact product. Your watchlist is remembered in this browser and can be shared as a link.

    Paste a list

    One item per line, or separated by commas or semicolons. A column copied from a spreadsheet works too. Each line is a keyword. To pin something exactly, write Supplier :: Product for one product, or supplier: Name for one supplier.

    Share or bookmark this view

    Anyone who opens this link sees the same list, without searching again. Bookmark it to come back to it yourself.

    What to include

    The vendor list is stored in the part of the link after #, which browsers don't send to the server. It is visible to anyone you share the link with. Tip: press Ctrl+D to bookmark this page.

    About PHP2List

    A searchable view of the software inventory published by NCSC-NL and contributors in the php2shell repository, licensed under EUPL-1.2. The list refreshes automatically from GitHub.

    The vulnerabilities were found by researchers at Wiz Research, OpenAI and Calif. Read the full story at php2shell.com and see our acknowledgements.

    Accuracy is not guaranteed; use at your own risk. No support is offered; list corrections go to NCSC-NL. See the disclaimer.

    Status labels

    • Investigation May be affected; being assessed.
    • Vulnerable Confirmed vulnerable in the stated scope.
    • Fix The listed version contains a fix.
    • Workaround Vulnerable; mitigation available.
    • Not vuln Confirmed not vulnerable.

    The Version column relates to the status: known vulnerable versions, or the version that introduced the fix.
