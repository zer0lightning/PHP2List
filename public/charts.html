Patch progress

How many listed products are fixed, and how many are still vulnerable or unresolved, since the PHP2Shell patches were disclosed.

How to read these charts

Each product is counted once, by the less resolved of its two CVE statuses. For example, "Fix" for one CVE and "Investigation" for the other counts as under investigation. Still open means vulnerable, workaround only, under investigation, or no status yet. The history is rebuilt from the NCSC-NL changelog. The list only covers products someone has added to it, so a rise in the total means the list grew, not that more products became vulnerable. Accuracy is not guaranteed.
