About PHP2List

A searchable view of the software inventory published by NCSC-NL and contributors in the php2shell repository, licensed under EUPL-1.2. The list refreshes automatically from GitHub.

The vulnerabilities were found by researchers at Wiz Research, OpenAI and Calif. Read the full story at php2shell.com and see our acknowledgements.

Accuracy is not guaranteed; use at your own risk. No support is offered; list corrections go to NCSC-NL. See the disclaimer.

Status labels

  • Investigation May be affected; being assessed.
  • Vulnerable Confirmed vulnerable in the stated scope.
  • Fix The listed version contains a fix.
  • Workaround Vulnerable; mitigation available.
  • Not vuln Confirmed not vulnerable.

The Version column relates to the status: known vulnerable versions, or the version that introduced the fix.
